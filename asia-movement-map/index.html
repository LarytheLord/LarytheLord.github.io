The sticky notes

Every note from the three regional boards, in each board's four columns, written as participants wrote it. Note colours are as on the paper and carry no meaning. A dashed outline marks words that are covered or faint. Use the pencil on any note to suggest a correction.

Who works on what

Each dot is an organisation from the boards, coloured by region, joined to the themes it works on. Groups on two or more boards have a second ring. A solid line comes from the group's own note; a dashed line comes from its public profile. Select a dot or a theme to see what it connects to.

Gaps, and who may already help

With "All themes", you see the 12 places where a gap written on one board meets work listed on the boards, and the gaps nobody listed work for. Pick a theme to see every gap and every piece of work on it, the groups behind them, and what published sources say.

Organisations on the boards

Every organisation written on a board, with what the notes say, a short public profile, and the gaps its work could help with. Spot a mistake about your group? Use "Suggest a correction" on its card.

Also at the summit, not on these boards

Groups on the summit attendee list that do not appear on the three boards, grouped by what their name suggests. Their relevance has not been checked with them.
